## Calculation note

The Fiverr comparison in `docs/charlie-offer.md` is an illustrative calculation from the cited public figures:

- five orders × $350 = $1,750 listed seller gross;
- $1,750 × 80% = approximately $1,400 seller earnings;
- $1,750 × 5.5% = $96.25 buyer percentage fee;
- buyer total = approximately $1,846.25 where no per-order under-$200 fee applies;
- difference between buyer total and seller earnings = approximately $446.25.

This is not a quote from Fiverr and does not include taxes, currency conversion, withdrawals, optional services, refunds, or later changes to fees.
