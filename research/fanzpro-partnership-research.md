## What FanzPro does

FanzPro (fanzpro.com) is a dual-sided web platform connecting
independent music artists with fans, bloggers, and affiliates who
act as a decentralized sales team. Artists set up offers (merch,
tickets, music releases, fan club memberships), define commission
rates (8% to 20%), and supporters receive trackable referral links.
Payouts trigger only on verified, completed sales. Infrastructure:
Stripe Connect for disbursements, W-9 onboarding for tax compliance,
local storage attribution codes for link tracking.

The platform is registered as FanzPro LLC, Nashville, TN. As of
research date, status is Live Beta v1.01.

CEO: Bryan Ogden. Source: linkedin.com/in/bryanogden1 and
bryanogden.com. Ogden describes his thesis as building technology
that helps real people be heard, verified, and connected. He has
30+ years building systems and describes his AI consulting practice
around trustworthy, privacy-first, human-in-the-loop deployment.

## What FanzPro does not address

FanzPro has no mechanism for:
- Documenting creator ownership of content before a sale occurs
- Recording rights validity at the point of a revenue event
- Generating structured dispute records for Content ID or platform
  monetization challenges
- Supporting a creator in a licensing negotiation with a major label

These gaps are the basis for the Creator Rights Layer module stub
(see docs/architecture/creator-rights-layer.md).

---

## Potential complementarity with 50 Ways

FanzPro generates verified sales and performance data. The 50 Ways
architecture generates provenance and rights documentation. A creator
who uses both would have: a documented ownership record, a trackable
performance record, and a paper trail that supports both a licensing
conversation and a Content ID dispute response.

This complementarity is architectural, not contractual. No
integration exists. No partnership has been proposed or agreed.
