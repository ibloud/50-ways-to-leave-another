## Exercise

Create one record per name: claimed owner and supporting instrument; earliest retrieved dated use (record the exact artifact and date, not an invented historical first); claimed commercial first use and evidence; actual/proposed goods/services; territories; exact name/logo versions; application/registration number, owner, status and official registry retrieval date **if verified**; similar-mark search terms/results and search date; third-party art/fonts/code and licenses; unresolved clearance decision and reviewer.

USPTO TM-01 recommends broader clearance than an exact federal-register match: consider confusingly similar names, related goods/services, common-law and other relevant sources. Record searches that failed and what scope remains. Registry absence alone cannot establish safety. Copyright in text/art/software and trademark identification of commercial source require separate analysis; registering one does not establish the other.
