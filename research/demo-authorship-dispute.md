## Why this belongs in 50 Ways to Leave Another

**Before using a demo, what would you need to establish—and what would you preserve?**

This is a project learning question, not a conclusion about the parties. It connects the site's existing work on contribution, permission, credit, compensation and the consequences of proceeding with missing evidence.

For an original fictional exercise, ask:

1. Which material existed before the collaboration, and which contributions were new?
2. Which version was delivered, to whom, when, and for what proposed use?
3. What evidence corroborates editable timestamps?
4. What did the participants actually agree about use, credit, payment and approval?
5. What work should pause while contribution or authority remains disputed?

The learner's task is to identify missing evidence and choose a documented next step. Do not use the exercise to assign a fictional verdict to real people.
