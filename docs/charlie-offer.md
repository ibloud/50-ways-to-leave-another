# Proposed Charlie J offer

> **Status:** nonbinding proposal. Charlie J is not attached to the project and has not endorsed this repository.

## Public-market baseline

Charlie's public Fiverr listing presents:

| Package | Public scope | Listed price |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Basic | 8-bar verse, 15–20 seconds | $70 |
| Standard | 16-bar verse, 30–40 seconds | $130 |
| Premium | Full song, two verses and hook, up to 180 seconds | $350 |

Fiverr states that a freelancer earns 80% of completed purchases. Fiverr also states that buyers pay a 5.5% service fee plus $3.50 on purchases below $200. Excluding tax and currency conversion, five $350 orders list at $1,750, pay the seller approximately $1,400, cost the buyer approximately $1,846.25, and create a combined platform difference of approximately $446.25.

## Proposed scope

- $500 paid pilot, credited toward the complete EP.
- $2,500 total for five songs when Dominique supplies the musical foundations.
- Up to $3,500 when substantial original instrumental production is added by written agreement.
- Eight-week schedule after acceptance and delivery of required inputs.
- Two consolidated revision rounds per song.
- Release masters, clean versions, instrumentals, a cappellas, aligned stems, lyrics, credits, metadata, and agreed editable sessions.
- Pre-existing material remains with its owner.
- Master ownership and permitted uses become effective only under the signed agreement and payment terms.
- Publishing is allocated song by song according to actual authorship.
- All third-party and AI elements are disclosed and cleared or replaced.

## Why ChatGPT is disclosed

ChatGPT/Codex was used to organize the archive, compare public rates with the complete assignment, calculate fee effects, identify missing rights and delivery terms, and implement the project record. It is not the songwriter, performer, rights holder, negotiator, or final decision-maker.
