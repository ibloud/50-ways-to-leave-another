# Black Film Market and 50 Ways to Leave Another

Black Film Market, presented by TruJuLo Productions, is developing a 2027 Los Angeles market and forum for financing Black-led stories. Its December 2026 online listening sessions invited filmmakers to bring financing roadblocks and help shape the market. [Organizer site](https://blackfilmmarket.com/)

[Explore the 50 Ways series pitch](../index.html#series) · [Read the original preparation notes](black-film-market-prep-archive.md)

<h2 id="prepare-heading">Prepare for the listening session</h2>
<p>Bring a short project introduction, one financing roadblock and a specific question. For 50 Ways, we want to learn what makes a Black-led, music-driven series ready for buyer or financier meetings and how to present its interactive proof of concept.</p>

<section data-bfm-follow-up hidden aria-labelledby="follow-up-heading">
<h2 id="follow-up-heading">Post-event follow-up plan</h2>
<p>Check the organizer’s recap or firsthand notes for answers about eligibility, buyer access, project materials and rights support. Record the source and date for each answer and make a list of questions still open.</p>
<p>Follow up with anyone who invited contact. Reference the conversation and ask whether they would like the one-pager or a short demo. Update the pitch package when the feedback identifies a concrete change, such as how to present the interactive proof or which financing milestone to document next.</p>
<p>Use the original prep notes to compare the questions we brought with answers we can substantiate.</p>
</section>
