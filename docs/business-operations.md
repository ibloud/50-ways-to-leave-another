# Business operations for junior producers

## Before work

Define parties, scope, deliverables, milestones, revisions, acceptance, cancellation, payment, credit, confidentiality, AI, ownership, and public use. Identify cost authority and subcontracting limits. Separate project and personal records. Establish invoice, receipt, expense, and reconciliation methods. Ask appropriate professionals about entity, tax, classification, depreciation, rights, publicity, youth, insurance, disputes, and governing law.

## During production

Maintain a decision log, version register, contribution record, budget-to-actual report, cash ledger, rights envelope, issue list, and backup verification. Chat history must not be the only operational record.

## At delivery

Confirm files, metadata, credits, approvals, invoices, payment, rights conditions, retention/deletion duties, and public naming. Record unresolved items.

Educational templates do not establish an entity, classify a worker, clear rights, create insurance, file taxes, or form an attorney-client relationship.
