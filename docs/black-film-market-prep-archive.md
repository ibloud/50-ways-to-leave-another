# Black Film Market preparation archive

Prepared November 26, 2026 for the free “Build It With Us” listening sessions. Fanshen Cox hosted the sessions on behalf of TruJuLo Productions. The organizer invited creators to help shape its planned 2027 Los Angeles market for financing Black-led stories. [Black Film Market](https://blackfilmmarket.com/)

## What to bring

Pick one project and one financing roadblock. Prepare a 20-second introduction with the format, audience, development stage and next milestone. Keep a one-pager or short teaser available if requested. If you are actively raising, know the budget, committed financing, remaining gap and which rights you control or still need to clear.

## What to ask

- What would make a project at this stage ready for a buyer or financier meeting?
- Who will qualify for curated meetings, and who will have funding or acquisition authority?
- Will creators get help comparing terms, including repayment and rights retention?
- Will the market track offers, signed deals and funding actually received?

For 50 Ways, ask how to present the interactive proof of concept alongside a proposed Black-led, music-driven scripted series. Is it best used to demonstrate the experience, or should it be introduced as a separate product opportunity?

## What a virtual session can yield

Aim for one concrete next step. A clear selection criterion, a useful peer contact or permission to follow up would count. The listening session is designed to gather input about the future market.
