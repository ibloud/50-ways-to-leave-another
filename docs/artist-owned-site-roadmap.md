# Artist-owned site roadmap

The site is a teaching demonstration and potential Charlie-owned professional home. It is not a condition of the EP commission.

## Ownership

The final domain, repository, analytics, mailing list, client information, and editorial control should belong to Charlie. Loptr Lab may build and document the architecture without taking silent ownership of his audience, identity, or music.

## Pages

| Page | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Position Charlie beyond one marketplace gig |
| Music / portfolio | Prove range with approved work and credits |
| Services | Separate writing, performance, production, delivery, and rights |
| EP production | Qualify higher-value custom projects |
| Case studies | Show process and results |
| Rights and working terms | Explain licenses, splits, revisions, and clearance |
| EPK | Provide biography, photos, credits, links, and press materials |
| Inquiry | Gather budget, schedule, references, deliverables, and requested rights |
| Journal | Publish artist-approved long-form work |

## ChatGPT demonstration

Every page should show a practical use without overstating automation: scope design, rate modeling, intake, rights questions, metadata, accessible copy, portfolio organization, or provenance. Charlie remains the author and approver of his professional identity.

## Standard.site

[Standard.site](https://standard.site/) is an AT Protocol publishing schema, not a conventional site builder or payment processor. Build the ordinary site first, then add Standard.site-compatible publication and document records to the journal and case-study layer.
