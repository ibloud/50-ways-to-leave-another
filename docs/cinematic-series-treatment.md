## The hook

A professional contract killer discovers that her lover has betrayed her with someone they both trusted. He knows exactly what she is capable of. Everyone expects revenge. Her most consequential choice is to refuse both retaliation and the assignment—and leave.

**Core question:** What happens when a person whose livelihood depends on control decides to stop allowing others to determine her choices?

**Format and audience — proposal:** a premium serialized thriller conceived as a limited series for adult audiences interested in psychological tension, intimate betrayal, agency and music-led storytelling. This is a positioning proposal, not evidence of audience demand, buyer interest, financing or a finalized episode order.

## The story: reclaiming the decision

**Foundation:** the protagonist earns her living through control. Her lover knowingly risks a relationship with someone accustomed to decisive action. Betrayal makes revenge the anticipated response; the trusted third person's role makes the breach intimate rather than abstract.

**Proposed dramatic movement:** discovery makes a familiar solution available. As she tests what action would actually reclaim her life, retaliation begins to resemble another task organized around other people's choices. Refusing the assignment alongside the relationship makes the ending an active withdrawal of consent. The suspense comes from whether she can stop performing the role that everyone—including herself—expects.

**Open development questions:** what the assignment is, how it intersects with the relationship, who knows about the betrayal, which consequences follow refusal, and how the final departure is dramatized. These need the owner's acceptance and writers' work. The story must earn independence through choices, not suggest that violence is a universal response to betrayal. No diagnosis or real-life analogue is assigned to any character.

Autonomy, identity, the economics of intimacy and the difference between power and freedom provide the thematic frame. Psychological tension and consequence are the promise; specific action sequences, locations and production scale remain undecided.
