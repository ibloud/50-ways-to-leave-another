# 50 Ways: production model for learning and review

**Open development and educational record · Independent professional review: PENDING**

The [portfolio pitch](https://ibloud.github.io/ecosystem/projects/50-ways/) presents the show. This separate [50 Ways project site](https://50ways.loptrlab.com/) is the behind-the-scenes working record: how the creative property could be developed, used, promoted and monetized, and what evidence would support those decisions.

This is a model to work through and examine, not a completed financing, rights-management or royalty-collection service. Participants bring a reviewable proposal, show their reasoning, identify missing evidence, receive feedback and revise. Professional experience and participant learning meet around the work. This is not an accredited qualification or institutional examination.

## Gods of Mayhem: the human review community

Gods of Mayhem means the contributors, professionals, experienced practitioners and thought leaders who have the time, resources and capacity to participate for the sake of the ecosystem. Their contribution is experience, questions, practical judgment and scoped review—not automatic authority granted by a symbol or software.

Participants choose what they can contribute and the scope they can sustain. Agree recognition, attribution, confidentiality, time and any compensation before work begins. Voluntary ecosystem contribution is a choice, not a requirement that everyone work without pay. Professional engagements, credentials and review scope are established separately where relevant.

The purpose is collaborative correction: identify misunderstandings early, preserve accurate records, support informed consent and allow reasonable opportunities to correct mistakes. Evidence protects everyone involved. Public comments, participation or a completed exercise do not certify legal compliance, approve a recording or endorse a pitch. No reviewer is named or enrolled automatically.

Use the existing [Legal & Rights review route](https://ibloud.github.io/legal-rights/#gods-of-mayhem-community) and [public correction discussion](https://github.com/ibloud/ibloud.github.io/issues/44) for public-safe questions and cited corrections. Keep personal financial, benefits, health, housing, identifying and legally sensitive records private within specifically authorized handling.

## A useful review submission

Prepare a public-safe question and a fictional or expressly approved example; link the source/version; distinguish facts, claims, proposals and unknowns; show the proposed process; name what would change the decision. Record reviewer scope and corrections without overwriting prior evidence. Review is an iterative learning process, not a blame assignment.

Use the existing [capstone and 24-point rubric](../ASSESSMENT-RUBRIC.md) where appropriate; there is no second grading system. A well-supported Hold is a valid professional outcome. The existing [PIXIE evidence specification](https://loptr-lab.github.io/pixie-ecosystem-guide/assets/evidence-workflow.html) and [Training sample exports](https://loptr-lab.github.io/training/legal-rights/) help make records portable; they do not activate private mail/storage or the held OAuth/Library candidates.

## Keep the two destinations clear

- **Portfolio:** [the show pitch and treatment](https://ibloud.github.io/ecosystem/projects/50-ways/), for story, creative vision and potential partners.
- **50 Ways:** this production, learning and review record, for economics, rights, royalty collection, promotion and collaboration questions.

The links connect the two. Neither replaces the other. No money, rights, professional approval or participant commitment is created by crossing between them.
