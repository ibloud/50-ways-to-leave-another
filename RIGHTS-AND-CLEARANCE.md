# Rights and clearance

No asset moves from concept to release merely because it appears in this repository.

## Rights layers

| Layer | Question | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Composition | Who wrote lyrics, melody, and other copyrightable musical material? | Contribution records and signed split sheet |
| Master | Who owns and may exploit the final recording? | Signed production/collaboration agreement and payment record |
| Performance | May each recorded performance be released and edited? | Performer consent and release terms |
| Beat / instrumental | Is the license valid for the intended release, territory, term, and revenue? | License copy and purchase/permission evidence |
| Samples / interpolations | Was third-party material identified and cleared? | Written clearance or documented replacement |
| Voice / likeness | May a name, image, biography, or voice be used publicly? | Specific written consent |
| AI output | What tool generated or transformed what element, under which terms? | Tool/version record, source record, disclosure, and terms review |
| Promotion / case study | May the collaborator and work be discussed publicly? | Separate approval of the named public use |

## Track clearance gate

For each track, complete the following before distribution:

- identify every contributor and source asset;
- distinguish pre-existing material from new work;
- complete contribution records and a split sheet totaling 100%;
- define master ownership, licenses, payment triggers, and revenue participation;
- confirm beat, sample, interpolation, and third-party permissions;
- record AI-generated or AI-transformed elements and applicable service terms;
- obtain performer, voice, likeness, credit, and promotional permissions;
- replace any element that cannot be documented;
- approve final credits, metadata, and delivery materials.

## Storage

Public GitHub holds only public-safe documentation. Protected storage holds source audio, stems, sessions, agreements, IDs, contact details, receipts, and unpublished work. Public files may refer to a stable private evidence locator but must not expose protected URLs or credentials.

## Stop conditions

Do not release, license, pitch as attached talent, train on a contributor, or publish a named case study when required evidence is missing or disputed. Escalate unresolved ownership or contract questions to qualified counsel.

This is project-control documentation, not legal advice.
