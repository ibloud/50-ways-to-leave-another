# Contribution record

## What you will make

Create one record for each person contributing to your practice project. Use
fictional details for the course. Keep a separate record when a person's role,
asset or terms change. This worksheet records proposed terms and evidence; it is
not a signed agreement, a license or a transfer of ownership.

## Complete the record

1. Download the Markdown source at the bottom of this page and open it in a text
   editor, or copy the blank template below into your project notes.
2. Replace each bracketed field. Write **unresolved** when you do not know; write
   **not applicable** only with a reason. Do not treat a proposal as agreement.
3. Connect the proposed fee to your budget, ownership questions to the split
   sheet, and intended uses to the rights envelope.
4. Save it in your project package as `contribution-[fictional-name]-v1`.
5. Review the record with the contributor before using actual work. Keep real
   names, contact details, agreements and approval evidence in private records.

## Blank template — copy and complete

```text
Project / track: [title]
Record version and date: [version; date]
Contributor / professional credit: [fictional name for practice]
Role and deliverables: [what this person will make; file/version]
Contribution date or milestone: [when]
Pre-existing or newly created: [describe]
Human-created elements: [describe]
AI tools and generated/transformed elements: [describe; none; or unresolved]
Source assets and provenance: [origin; evidence reference; unresolved questions]
Composition ownership: [proposed share; agreed evidence; or unresolved]
Master ownership: [separate proposed share; evidence; or unresolved]
Publishing administration / revenue participation: [separate from ownership]
Performer / producer credit wording: [proposed wording]
Fee and payment dates: [amount/currency; deposit/balance; status]
Scope changes / revisions: [included work; how extra work is approved]
Permitted uses: [specific purpose; territory; term; proposal/agreement status]
Excluded uses: [what has not been permitted]
Voice / likeness / AI-training permission: [separate status for each]
Cancellation / exit / expiration: [proposed terms; review required]
Approval evidence location: [private reference; none yet]
Contributor confirmation: [pending; or actual dated evidence reference]
Open questions and next action: [question; responsible person; next step]
```

## Check your work and continue

Done for this exercise means every field contains an answer or a clearly labelled
unresolved question; proposed fees match the budget; the split sheet distinguishes
composition from master ownership; and the rights envelope matches intended uses.
Do not mark a real contribution approved without evidence.

Continue with the [split sheet](split-sheet.md), [rights envelope](rights-envelope.md)
and [assessment rubric](../ASSESSMENT-RUBRIC.md). Bring unresolved questions to
qualified help before a rights-dependent action. A completed worksheet alone does
not authorize use of someone's work.
