## Approval gates

- [ ] Contributor payments and reimbursements due before purchase are funded.
- [ ] Tax/dispute reserve was set with appropriate professional input.
- [ ] Restricted funds permit this use.
- [ ] Full lifecycle—not merely purchase—cost is recorded.
- [ ] Rental, maker-space, and public-access alternatives were priced.
- [ ] Accessibility and transportation costs are included.
- [ ] Youth participation has safeguarding, consent, privacy, insurance, and ownership terms.
- [ ] No contingent Netflix, licensing, royalty, grant, or sponsorship income is treated as cash.
- [ ] The decision has a review date and exit condition.
