## Evidence-status legend

- **Verified:** the specified fact/evidence is checked; this does not by itself mean the work is cleared.
- **Unresolved:** necessary information remains unknown or disputed.
- **Hold:** pause the identified rights-dependent action while the named evidence/review is missing.
- **Cleared by documented authorization:** only for a specified version/use after competent review of sufficient authority; never an automatic score or status change.

## Before a soundtrack or sync transaction

- [ ] Identify exact composition, recording, render, intended use, media, territory, term and promotional/editing scope.
- [ ] Verify both title chains, all writers/performers/producers and any transfers/licenses; retain supporting evidence privately.
- [ ] Inspect the reported Paul Simon drum-source question in authorized session backups and each candidate render. Determine what is present, from which source, what was approved and what authorization covers it. Do not assume every version contains it or decide infringement from a summary.
- [ ] Evaluate sample/master and composition/interpolation permissions separately, plus performance, voice/likeness, privacy, credit, promotion and any third-party assets.
- [ ] Determine whether original cue use or an existing recording is proposed; align sync and master-use scope with the actual buyer agreement. No universal buyer format or acceptance is promised.
- [ ] Document human/AI contributions, source inputs, plan/feature dates, applicable terms and any permitted export evidence.
- [ ] Negotiate ownership, licensing/exclusivity, fees/royalties, recoupment, accounting, approvals, credit, revisions and exit. Do not invent splits or treat expected revenue as cash.
- [ ] Confirm deliverables: agreed master specification, clean/instrumental/dialogue versions as needed, aligned stems, agreed editable sessions, credits, metadata and acceptance record.
- [ ] Verify the receiving entity's submission policy and authorized representatives; no unsolicited access or endorsement is implied.
- [ ] Obtain competent review and a use-specific authorization record before changing Hold to Cleared by documented authorization.

## Before publishing production history

- [ ] Distinguish creator request, engineer response, approved changes, interpretation and unresolved facts.
- [ ] Preserve complete threads and qualifying evidence privately. Publish only specifically approved, nonconfidential summaries.
- [ ] Offer an appropriate opportunity to clarify/correct a material misunderstanding; record responses without assuming motive.
- [ ] Keep payment reconciliation, quality acceptance and legal clearance separate.
- [ ] Do not publish identities, correspondence, private financial/health/benefit information or protected media without the necessary permission.

**Copyable decision:** version/use; status; evidence/source/date; unresolved question; responsible role; response/correction opportunity; next action; reviewer and scope; agreed authorization reference. Passing the existing curriculum does not authorize a track or certify compliance.
