## Split Definitions

**Songwriter %** — Share of publishing income (performance royalties, mechanical royalties, sync fees). Registered with each contributor's PRO (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, etc.).

**Master %** — Share of master recording income (streaming, downloads, master sync). Routed through Stem.

**Producer %** — Point-based producer share of master income, if separate from Master %. Enter 0 if producer share is folded into Master %.

> If Songwriter % and Master % differ for any contributor, note the reason in the Notes section below.

## Payment Infrastructure

### Stem (Master Royalty Distribution)
All master royalties are routed automatically via **Stem** (stem.is). Each contributor must:
1. Create a free Stem account at [stem.is](https://stem.is) using the email listed in the Contributor Table above.
2. Complete identity verification (government ID + bank account or PayPal).
3. Confirm receipt of the Stem collaboration invite sent by Loptr Lab before the distribution date.

Stem distributes on a **monthly** cycle, approximately 45–60 days after the royalty period closes (consistent with distributor reporting windows). Contributors will receive an itemized statement via Stem each cycle.

### Publishing / PRO (Songwriter Royalties)
Each contributor registers their songwriter share directly with their PRO (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, or international equivalent). Loptr Lab does not collect or distribute publishing royalties on behalf of contributors — each party is responsible for their own PRO registration for this work.

> If a contributor does not have a PRO affiliation, they should register at ascap.com or bmi.com before this sheet is countersigned.

### Session Fees (Non-Royalty Contributors)
Contributors receiving a flat session fee in lieu of a royalty split will be paid via **Stripe** (invoice) or **PayPal** within **14 days of final approved delivery** of their contribution. Session fee recipients do not appear in the royalty split table unless separately negotiated.
