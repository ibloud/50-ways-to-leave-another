# Facilitator guide

Use this package in a library, maker space, public-access facility, school, nonprofit, or peer-learning group.

## Session structure

1. State the learning goal and access needs.
2. Use the fictional capstone before participant-owned projects.
3. Separate public examples from private evidence.
4. Complete one small artifact per session.
5. End with unresolved questions and one safe next action.

## Rules

Do not require disclosure of trauma, legal status, disability, finances, or unpublished work. Offer text, audio, visual, printable, and low-bandwidth routes. Do not turn critique into ownership or unpaid labor. Define whether work is practice, portfolio, commissioned, or collaborative. Never present a facilitator or AI as legal, tax, mental-health, or safeguarding counsel. Apply YOUTH-SAFETY-AND-ACCESS.md for minors.

Measure completion, paid opportunities, portfolio readiness, accessible participation, rights literacy, referrals, and participant-defined outcomes—not views or follower counts.
