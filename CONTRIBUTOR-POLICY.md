# Contributor policy

A contributor must know what is being requested, whether the role is paid under a signed agreement or voluntary, what rights are requested, how AI may be used, and what will be public before work begins.

## Commitments

- Persistent, accurate credit.
- Written scope, milestones, revision limits, and delivery standards.
- Payment for commissioned work according to its signed agreement; voluntary contributions carry no promise of pay.
- Song-by-song authorship and publishing decisions.
- No private evidence or unpublished work in the public repository without permission.
- No model training on voice, likeness, stems, sessions, or unreleased work without affirmative consent.
- No out-of-scope sublicensing.
- A correction and attribution process.
- A documented exit path for unfinished or rejected work.
- Public case-study participation is optional and separate from the music commission.

## Naming prospective collaborators

Public sources may be analyzed, but this repository must not imply that a prospective collaborator accepted, endorsed, performed on, or co-owns the project. Named participation requires review and consent.

## Adaptive participation

Ask about functional barriers at the start and agree accessible alternatives, including text-first or asynchronous work. Technology should adapt to the participant. Participation must not require disability disclosure or biometric movement data. Movement data has its own permissions: no secondary use or AI training without appropriate rights and explicit authorisation. Preserve fair compensation for commissioned professional work and distinguish it from optional learning or voluntary playtesting. Use the existing contribution and rights records; no new assessment or certification is created.
