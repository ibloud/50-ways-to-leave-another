PIXIE v0.1 · Experimental ATProto Discovery Prototype

Find something outside your usual neighborhood.

Pixie reads public ATProto data and makes bounded, explainable suggestions. Recommendations are inferences and may be wrong; they are not facts, endorsements, compatibility judgments, or claims about a person's identity or interests. Pixie does not automatically follow, post, reply, introduce, or contact anyone. Dismiss or stop at any time.
