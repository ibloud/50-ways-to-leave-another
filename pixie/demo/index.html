Find something outside your usual neighborhood.

Pixie reads public ATProto data and makes bounded, explainable suggestions. Recommendations are inferences and may be wrong; they are not facts, endorsements, compatibility judgments, or claims about a person's identity or interests. Pixie does not automatically follow, post, reply, introduce, or contact anyone. Dismiss or stop at any time.

Find the story in an author’s feed.

Search one public Bluesky author feed by words, phrase, and date. Select the posts you want to use and export them as a story ledger.

Open Story Finder Open Creator OS

In Creator OS, open Atmosphere and import the ledger from Files. Choose where the story belongs, add your context, and review its public representation. Routing currently uses local simulations; live publishing is not connected.

Story Finder opens as a separate tool. Exporting a selection does not publish it or grant consent to reshare.
