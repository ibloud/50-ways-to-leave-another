# Junior producer assessment rubric

Score each area 0–2: **0 missing**, **1 partial**, **2 complete and evidenced**. Pass at 20/24 with no failed stop condition.

| Area | Two-point standard |
| --- | --- |
| Function and scope | Audience, format, purpose, exclusions, and status are explicit |
| Creative architecture | Music/story relationship and approval authority are usable |
| Provenance | Facts, creator statements, third-party claims, inference, and uncertainty are separated |
| Contributor fairness | Roles, consent, credit, payment, revisions, exit, and public naming are addressed |
| Rights | Composition, master, performance, samples, AI, publicity, and promotion are tracked |
| Budget | Assumptions, included costs, contingencies, and approvals are visible |
| Cash timing | Cash, receivables, reported income, and contingent income are distinct |
| Capital decision | Buy/rent/share/community paths and lifecycle costs are compared |
| Delivery | Versions, stems, metadata, credits, acceptance, and storage are defined |
| Accessibility | Non-video, transcript, keyboard, contrast, and low-bandwidth needs are covered |
| Platform independence | Identity, export, backup, audience portability, and redirects are planned |
| Reflection | Unresolved risks, professional questions, and next action are honest |

No pass if private material is exposed, attachment is implied, forecasts become cash, contributors are omitted, uncleared material is called cleared, or minors lack safeguards.


## AI-assisted release: Check your work

Apply the existing 0–2 scale and 20/24 threshold; this adds no points or automated grading. This is learner self-check guidance for the [release practice](exercises/fictional-capstone.md#ai-assisted-release-decision), available for educator review. The classroom software's role/permission checks do not grade these judgments or grant release clearance.

| Existing area | Evidence needed for its two-point standard in this practice |
| --- | --- |
| Creative architecture | Compare both candidates against the same mood/audience, dialogue, structure/ending, and artifact criteria; explain the musical choice with observations |
| Provenance | Record both tools, versions, plans, sources, document dates, and check date; distinguish training disclosures from output terms, documented facts from claims and unknowns |
| Rights | Identify applicable output-use limits and input permission; track unresolved permission, credit, and compensation; name specific evidence needed to resolve a hold |
| Delivery | Link the evidence record and separate release decision to the existing delivery package; do not present a held track as approved |
| Reflection | Explain proceed, hold, or choose another option using evidence and a feasible next action |

For these areas, **0** means the relevant evidence is missing; **1** means it is partial (for example, “rights unclear” without a source or next step); **2** means complete and evidenced as above. Other rubric areas still apply to the full capstone. A properly evidenced hold can earn full credit. Calling uncleared material cleared still fails the existing stop condition.
