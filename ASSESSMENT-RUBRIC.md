# Junior producer assessment rubric

Score each area 0–2: **0 missing**, **1 partial**, **2 complete and evidenced**. Pass at 20/24 with no failed stop condition.

| Area | Two-point standard |
| --- | --- |
| Function and scope | Audience, format, purpose, exclusions, and status are explicit |
| Creative architecture | Music/story relationship and approval authority are usable |
| Provenance | Facts, creator statements, third-party claims, inference, and uncertainty are separated |
| Contributor fairness | Roles, consent, credit, payment, revisions, exit, and public naming are addressed |
| Rights | Composition, master, performance, samples, AI, publicity, and promotion are tracked |
| Budget | Assumptions, included costs, contingencies, and approvals are visible |
| Cash timing | Cash, receivables, reported income, and contingent income are distinct |
| Capital decision | Buy/rent/share/community paths and lifecycle costs are compared |
| Delivery | Versions, stems, metadata, credits, acceptance, and storage are defined |
| Accessibility | Non-video, transcript, keyboard, contrast, and low-bandwidth needs are covered |
| Platform independence | Identity, export, backup, audience portability, and redirects are planned |
| Reflection | Unresolved risks, professional questions, and next action are honest |

No pass if private material is exposed, attachment is implied, forecasts become cash, contributors are omitted, uncleared material is called cleared, or minors lack safeguards.
