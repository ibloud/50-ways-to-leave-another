# PLYR / Suno listening test

## Question

How do listeners in the ATmosphere respond to a disclosed AI-generated recording whose concept, source lyrics, narrative direction, and selection are human-authored?

This is not a hidden detection test. Concealment would test deception. Disclosure tests informed response.

## Listen to the comparison

- [Human source recording on Audio.com](https://audio.com/ibloud-ivxx/audio/50-ways-to-leave-another-mix-2-11-7-24)
- [Suno-assisted interpretation on PLYR](https://plyr.fm/track/1117?ref=7-KpL9yQ)

The Audio.com item is the canonical source recording. The PLYR item is the disclosed generated interpretation. Compare creative choices, production, emotional effect, and the platforms' presentation—not merely whether a recording used AI.

## Required track label

> **Human-authored, AI-generated demo.** Dominique Devereaux created the concept, source lyrics, and narrative direction. Suno generated the audible demo performance and production. This is a disclosed pre-production experiment, not a human vocal performance, finished commercial master, or autonomous AI authorship claim.

If a human-created melody, instrumental, or recording is later verified, add that contribution precisely. Do not call the composition entirely human-composed without evidence of human-created musical structure.

## Short PLYR description

> Human-authored, AI-generated demo. I created the concept, source lyrics, and narrative direction; Suno generated the audible performance and production. Shared transparently to learn how the ATmosphere responds before a human-produced EP version is developed.

## What to observe

- Does disclosure affect willingness to listen?
- Do listeners distinguish authorship from performance?
- Do listeners request a human-performed version?
- Which elements do they believe require human collaboration?
- Does provenance increase trust?
- What criticism concerns the work itself versus the production method?

## Ethics

- Do not imply a human performed synthetic vocals.
- Do not imply Suno autonomously authored Dominique's source material.
- Do not manipulate listeners with a concealed AI reveal.
- Do not collect or publish personal information.
- Treat likes and replies as contextual signals, not representative research.
- Quote or identify respondents only with permission.
- Preserve material corrections in the repository.

## Relationship to Charlie

The demo is a concept artifact, not Charlie's performance and not a target he must imitate. A prospective human collaborator may challenge, replace, reinterpret, or reject synthetic choices within the agreed brief. No model may be trained on a collaborator's voice or likeness without separate permission.
