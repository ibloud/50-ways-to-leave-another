The project

Infrastructure for better human collaboration

Dominique Devereaux / Loptr Lab is turning a private creative archive into a commissionable five-song EP. This repository makes the business and production system inspectable: scope, compensation, authorship, AI use, delivery, rights and corrections.

ChatGPT/Codex helps organize information, expose missing terms and preserve decisions. Human contributors retain authorship, consent, credit and final creative judgment.

Operating principles

01

Human-led

People create, perform, interpret, approve and receive credit for their actual work.

02

AI-assisted

Tools support accessibility, research, structure and documentation—not invisible replacement.

03

Rights-aware

Every contribution, source asset, permission, split and delivery condition must be recorded.

04

Growth-oriented

A contributor can coordinate specialists and build a repeatable production network without pretending to do everything.

Hiring infrastructure

A marketplace is a door—not the artist's whole house

Platforms can make a first collaboration safer and easier. They can also fragment reputation, compress complex work into a small package and keep the relationship inside someone else's system.

Verified Charlie listing

Fiverr

For Charlie: mature reviews, discovery and order handling; 20% of each completed order is retained under Fiverr's published seller-earnings model.

For a client: fast comparison, packaged scope and platform support; a package can hide whether writing, performance, stems, exclusivity and publishing are included.

Verified Charlie listing

AirGigs

For Charlie: a music-specific audience and a public $200 UK rap-vocal offer. AirGigs says seller commission varies from 8–15% and joining has no membership fee.

For a client: music-focused search, samples, reviews and protected payment; buyers pay 4.7% or at least $5, and each gig still needs a rights check.

Verified artist presence

Bandcamp

For Charlie: a catalog, direct fan relationship and evidence of released work. It presents him as an artist, not merely a service provider.

For a client: useful creative due diligence and a contact route; it is not a commissioning workflow, escrow service or substitute for an agreement.

Comparison · not verified for Charlie

SoundBetter

For talent: positioning among producers, singers, songwriters and engineers, with proposals and verified reviews.

For a client: project posting, specialist comparison, funded jobs and a record of exchanges. Current fees and provider eligibility require account-level confirmation.

Comparison · not verified for Charlie

Vocalizr

For talent: vocalist/producer discovery, collaborative workspaces and professional verification pathways.

For a client: a vocal-specific hiring pool and platform records. Its workflow is narrower than the complete EP coordination required here.

Recommended anchor

Artist-owned site

For Charlie: one durable identity joining portfolio, services, EPK, rights language, case studies and inquiry intake—without discarding marketplace discovery.

For a client: the clearest place to understand the artist before selecting a protected marketplace or direct contract appropriate to the job.

Recommendation: keep Fiverr and AirGigs as verified lead and transaction channels; use Bandcamp as proof of artistry; evaluate SoundBetter or Vocalizr only if they reach a distinct client segment; build an artist-owned site as the canonical professional home. Compare the artist's net pay, the client's total cost, dispute protection, rights defaults, review portability and relationship control—not sticker price alone.

The question

Are people turning to AI because they value artists less—or because creative work too often arrives without clear scope, complete delivery or durable rights records?

This project argues for better-supported humans: clearer agreements, accurate credit, paid coordination and tools that make the work legible.
